Assessment of learning will include a Practicum Proposal, the design and implementation of lesson plans, and reflective practice assignments (discussions and final paper).

Experiential learning integrates scholarly research and prior learning with practical experience in the practicum setting. All work submitted should reflect scholarly thinking and reflective practice, and should incorporate scholarly work with proper citations. APA format is required.

Assignment 1: Practicum Proposal

Introduction

Write a 3-5 page Practicum Proposal including the practicum site, practicum mentor, personal learning outcomes, and planned schedule for practicum. This practicum requires a minimum of 20 hours of experiential learning, including 10 hours of classroom observation (online or face-to-face), and a minimum of 10 hours dedicated to designing lesson plans and facilitating learning (combined). Include a discussion of how this practicum will contribute to your learning and career goals.

Instructions

After identifying a practicum site, write a 3-5 page Practicum Proposal, including the following sections:

  1. Practicum Site: Include a description of your practicum site. This should include details such as whether you will be observing a specific professor, teacher, or trainer, and the context for your observation and facilitation. For example, will you be observing a professor and then facilitating live discussions in her onsite class? Note: Although you need to identify your site for this assignment, you should consider whether you want to keep the site anonymous for your discussions in the Practicum Course. If so, be sure to indicate in your proposal how you will avoid identifying details in course discussions.
  2. Practicum Mentor: Include the signed Mentor Agreement.
  3. Learning Outcomes: Include three personal learning outcomes that describe what you want to learn in this practicum. These learning outcomes will shape your experiential learning in this practicum.
  4. Planned Schedule for 20 Hours of Experiential Learning, to include:
    • Observation of Teaching (Minimum of 10 hours): Include details about when and where you will observe teaching. Be sure to coordinate this now with those you hope to observe.
    • Designing and Facilitating Lessons/Classes (Minimum of 10 hours, Minimum of 3 Lessons). Include details about the three lessons you plan to design. Include details about the lessons you plan to facilitate.
    • Facilitating Online or Face-to-Face Discussions: Include details about where and when you plan to facilitate these discussions.
  5. Learning: An analysis of how this practicum will contribute to the achievement of practicum learning outcomes. Be specific. Explain how each element of the practicum will contribute to the personal learning outcomes you developed.
  6. Career Planning: A discussion of how this practicum will build on your current academic, personal, and professional knowledge, and how it will contribute to your career goals. Be specific.

The Practicum Proposal should be written in APA format.

Assignment Submission

Submit your Practicum Proposal in the Assessment Dropbox in Moodle for your instructor to review. The Practicum Proposal is due prior to the start of the Practicum course, usually 1-2 weeks in advance.

The Practicum Proposal is worth 10% of your course grade.

This is a required assignment. You cannot begin any onsite or online elements of this course until you receive approval from your professor.

Instructions

Your portfolio will include the Classroom Observation Logs, Facilitation Log, and Verification of Hours, as well as Lesson Plans you designed during the practicum. You will also include new writing, in which you synthesize your learning in this practicum, drawing on the reflections you posted in your reflective practice discussions. Finally, you will evaluate and analyze your learning in the practicum experience, considering the learning outcomes you set for yourself at the beginning of the practicum.

As you write your paper, be sure to use APA format. You will draw on Gibbs’ (1988) Reflective Cycle as you reflect on your learning and growth as a facilitator

Discussions

Reflective Practice Discussions

Experiential learning provides a rich opportunity for you to integrate course learning by facilitating learning experiences in an organization or classroom. As part of this process, you will engage in reflective practice.

Field Notes: As part of reflective practice, you will keep regular field notes of their practicum experience, including specific observations, interview notes, descriptions of experiences, etc.

“Field notes are the primary way of capturing the data that is collected from participant observations. Notes taken to capture this data include records of what is observed, including informal conversations with participants, records of activities and ceremonies, during which the researcher is unable to question participants about their activities, and journal notes that are kept on a daily basis” (Kawulich, 2005).

Written Reflection: Experiential learning is strongest when it synthesizes practical experience with prior knowledge and academic literature. Writing can be a powerful learning experience, so in this course you will engage in written reflection about your experiences, sharing your thoughts in class discussions.

Discussions: Through these discussions, you will engage in reflective practice, including reflection and critical analysis of your practicum experience, organizing and integrating new knowledge with prior knowledge through regular writing practice.

You will complete Reflective Practice Discussions using a reflective practice model you select.

Discussion 1: Reflective Practice – Experiential Learning

Instructions

Write a discussion post including the following:

  • Describe the context in which you will conduct your experiential learning (practicum). (Consider whether you want or need to keep the site anonymous and, if so, be sure to avoid identifying details.)
  • List the three personal learning outcomes you developed for your practicum experience (from your Practicum Proposal). For each learning outcome, specifically describe how you will assess your own learning. (In other words, how will you know if you’ve learned what you hope to learn?)
  • Building on what you learned in previous courses, share some reflective thoughts about how you hope to integrate your identity, values, and beliefs as a teacher/facilitator into your practicum experience.

Respond to the posts of two other learners, providing reflective feedback on their learning outcomes, practicum experience, and/or their integration of their identity, values, and beliefs with their practicum plans.

Discussion 3: Reflective Practice - Classroom Observation (Part 1)

Introduction

Reflective Practice Discussion 3 should be submitted once you have completed approximately half of your observation hours.

Instructions

Observe: Observe a teacher/facilitator for a minimum of 10 hours.

Journal: As you conduct your observations, keep handwritten notes, completing the observation form you select.

Reflect: After each observation, take a few minutes to reflect in your journal, using Gibbs’ Reflective Cycle as your guide:

  • Description – What happened? Briefly describe the session or activity you observed.
  • Feelings – What were your reactions? How did the facilitator’s approach make you feel? Curious, inspired, challenged? Based on what you observed, how did the student feel?
  • Evaluation – What was effective or not? What specific actions did the facilitator take to engage learners, create community, or communicate content clearly?
  • Analysis – Why did it work (or not)? Consider the strategies, context, or facilitator skills that made a difference.
  • Conclusion – What did you learn? Identify key takeaways about facilitation and creating meaningful learning experiences.
  • Action Plan – What will you try next? List 2–3 strategies you want to experiment with in your own teaching or facilitation.

Keep it brief—just enough to capture insights and ideas for your growth.

Discussion Posts: Write discussion post, including the following:

  • Share a photo of your journal page.
  • Based on your observation, analyze the lesson plan design, learning activities, and assessment strategies you observe in your classroom observation.
  • Discuss which (if any) principles of adult education and communities of learning were implemented during the learning experience.
  • List 2–3 strategies you want to experiment with in your own facilitation. Explain these strategies in detail and explain why you think they would be effective.
  • At the end of your discussion, pose an open-ended question to other practicum learners. Respond to the posts of two other learners, expanding on their experience, use of reflective practice, and/or asking a question about their experience.
