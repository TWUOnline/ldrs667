### Assessment

- The assessment for this unit includes the design of three lesson plans. For each lesson plan, you will complete the following:
1. **Design** 3 lesson plans, following the Lesson Plan Template
- Lesson Plan 1: Authentic and Inclusive Learning Experiences
- Lesson Plan 2: Cultural Competency
- Lesson Plan 3: Critical and Creative Thinking

2. **Post** your draft Lesson Plan in a discussion post for feedback from your instructor and other learners.

3. **Incorporating feedback** from other learners, instructor, and practicum supervisor, submit your final Lesson Plan in the Assignment area.
