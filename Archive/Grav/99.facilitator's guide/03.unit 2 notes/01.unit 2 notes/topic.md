# **Unit 2: Classroom Observation**

Read the Unit 2 overview, student learning outcomes, topics, activity checklist, and assessments in Moodle to familiarize yourself with student workload expectations.

**Facilitated Learning Lab Activity 2.3: Format for Classroom Observation**

In small groups, ask students to review classroom observation forms. Discuss the benefits and drawbacks of each form, and encourage students to select which form they will use to complete Activity 2.4.

**Reflective Practice Discussions 3 & 4: Classroom Observation**

After the learning lab is done, remind students they need to work on their unit assessments at home. See Moodle for a full explanation of what is expected, including a rubric for assessment, and walk students through the assignment description and success criteria.
