# **Unit 4: Teaching/Facilitating**

Read the Unit 4 overview, student learning outcomes, topics, activity checklist, and assessments in Moodle to familiarize yourself with student workload expectations.

**Facilitated Learning Lab Activity 4.1: Teaching/Facilitating**

As time allows, ask students to practice teaching/facilitating with colleagues. This can be done in an online or face-to-face environment. Each student can teach/facilitate a short (10-15 minute) lesson to colleagues posing as students, then invite constructive feedback from colleagues.

**Reflective Practice Discussions 8, 9, & 10: Teaching/Facilitating**

After the learning lab is done, remind students they need to work on their unit assessments at home. See Moodle for a full explanation of what is expected, including a rubric for assessment, and walk students through the assignment description and success criteria.
