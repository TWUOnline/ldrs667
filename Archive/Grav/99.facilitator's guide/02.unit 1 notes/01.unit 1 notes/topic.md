# **Unit 1: Developing a Reflective Practice**

Read the Unit 1 overview, student learning outcomes, topics, activity checklist, and assessments in Moodle to familiarize yourself with student workload expectations.

**Facilitated Learning Lab Activity 1.1: Read and Reflect**

Lead a group discussion about how to engage in reflective practice through each practicum element: classroom observation, lesson plan design, and facilitating/teaching. Encourage students to reflect on adult learning theory principles (andragogy) from the Mirriam (2001) reading.

**Topic 2: Gibb’s Reflective Cycle (1988)**

Discuss the role of Gibb’s Reflective Cycle in personal and professional practice and reflection. Invite students to share past experiences and future expectations for how reflective practices enhance personal and professional learning.

**Reflective Practice Discussions 1 & 2: Experiential Learning and Applying a Reflective Practice Model**

After the learning lab is done, remind students they need to work on their unit assessments at home. See Moodle for a full explanation of what is expected, including a rubric for assessment, and walk students through the assignment description and success criteria.
