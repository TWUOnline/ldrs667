#**Unit 3: Designing Learning Experiences**

Read the Unit 3 overview, student learning outcomes, topics, activity checklist, and assessments in Moodle to familiarize yourself with student workload expectations.

**Facilitated Learning Lab Activity 3.2: Prepare for Lesson Plan 1: Authentic and Inclusive Learning Experiences**

With the large group, discuss the three learning community models using the Jigsaw Method. Please review the [Jigsaw Method video](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=euhtXUgBEts) in preparation for facilitating this activity. Assign students to one of three groups and ask them to share notes about their “expert group” topics during Round 1 of discussions, then share with their “learning groups” in Round 2 of discussions. Substantive participation is encouraged.

**Facilitated Learning Lab Activity 3.3: Prepare for Lesson Plan 2: Cultural Competency**

Invite students to share teaching strategies relating to culturally inclusive learning experiences. This can be done in large group discussion, small group discussion, or using the collaborative whiteboard provided.

**Facilitated Learning Lab Activity 3.4: Prepare for Lesson Plan 3: Creative and Critical Thinking**

Invite students to share teaching strategies relating to creative and critical thinking learning experiences. This can be done in large group discussion, small group discussion, or using the collaborative whiteboard provided.

**Assessment of Learning: Lesson Plan Design 1, 2, & 3 DRAFT and FINAL; Reflective Practice Discussions 5, 6, & 7: Designing Learning Experiences**

After the learning lab is done, remind students they need to work on their unit assessments at home. See Moodle for a full explanation of what is expected, including a rubric for assessment, and walk students through the assignment description and success criteria. Ensure students understand where to find lesson plan templates and rubrics.
