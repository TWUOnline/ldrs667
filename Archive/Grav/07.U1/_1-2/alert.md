#- Read and Reflect

- Read: Larrivee, B. (2000). [Transforming teaching practice: Becoming the critically reflective teacher](http://ed253jcu.pbworks.com/w/page/f/Larrivee_B_2000CriticallyReflectiveTeacher.pdf). *Reflective Practice,* *1*(3), 293–307.

- Read: Forrest, M. E. S. (2008). On becoming a critically reflective practitioner. *Health Information & Libraries Journal, 25*(3), 229-232. Article can be accessed through the [TWU Library](https://www.twu.ca/library/).
