## ASSESSMENT of LEARNING

Assessment of learning will include a Practicum Proposal, the design and
implementation of lesson plans, and reflective practice assignments (discussions
and final paper).

Experiential learning integrates scholarly research and prior learning with
practical experience in the practicum setting. All work submitted should reflect
scholarly thinking and reflective practice, and should incorporate scholarly
work with proper citations. APA format is required.
