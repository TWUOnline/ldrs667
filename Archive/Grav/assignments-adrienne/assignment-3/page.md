## Assessment of Learning

### Lesson Plan Design and Implementation

Learners will complete a minimum of 10 hours of lesson plan design and
implementation, including the design of three lesson plan and the facilitation
of three lesson plans. (Ideally, learners will facilitation the lesson plans
they design, although that may not always be possible.) Learners will use the
Lesson Plan Template for each lesson plan, which includes: Lesson Topic, Learning Outcomes, Learning Activities, and Learning Assessment. Each unit will
include a specific focus *(LP1: Authentic/Inclusive Learning, LP2:
Culturally-Inclusive Communication, LP3: Creative/Critical Thinking)* and will
include 1) one of the six principles of the Community of Learning (Boyer, 1990),
and 2) a specific adult learning theory.
